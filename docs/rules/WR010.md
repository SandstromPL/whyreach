# WR010: the destination is still starting (a startup race)

- **Layer:** 1, destination
- **Confidence:** high when the healthcheck hasn't passed yet and nothing listens on the port; medium when its listeners can't be read, or when it has no healthcheck, started less than a minute ago and listens on nothing
- **Supersedes:** WR001, WR003
- **Since:** v0.1 (M2)

## The pitfall

`depends_on` without a condition only waits for the destination's container to *start*, not for its server to *listen*. A database that needs ten seconds to initialise, or an API that runs migrations first, refuses the client's first connection. Clients that connect once at startup then crash or give up:

```
ECONNREFUSED 172.22.0.3:5432   # only on the first try
```

## The evidence

| Evidence | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `docker.container` of the destination | it runs, its health is `starting`, and `StartedAt` compared with when it was inspected |
| `netns.listeners` of the destination | nothing listens on the port yet (Docker's embedded-DNS listener aside) |
| `probe.tcp` from the source (optional) | refused: confirms it |

## The fix

- Give the destination a healthcheck, and make the client wait for it: in Compose, `depends_on: {api: {condition: service_healthy}}` on the client.
- Make the client retry its first connection anyway: services restart, and healthchecks can pass before the port is really ready.

## False positives and limits

- Without a healthcheck whyreach can't tell "still starting" from "never going to listen". It fires only when the container started less than a minute before the check and listens on nothing at all; after that, an empty listener table is WR001.
- A destination that already listens on the port (even on loopback only) has started, whatever its health says: that is WR002 or no problem.
- A destination that listens on another port has started too: that is WR003.
- An `unhealthy` or crash-looping destination is WR008's case.
- With replicas behind one name, a starting replica fails only the connections that reach it: when another replica serves, WR010 is one level less confident and says which ones serve. The answer doesn't depend on the order of the replicas.
